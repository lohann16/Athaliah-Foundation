About Us

Our story, our purpose, our people

Athaliah Foundation's identity is rooted in service, dignity and empowerment, with a special commitment to widows, widowers, orphans, vulnerable children and disadvantaged households.

Carmelle Nady Doualla Ekwe
Founder & President
Message From The Founder

Compassion, turned into action

Athaliah Foundation was born from a deeply personal journey and a calling to serve. The life and memory of Athaliah continue to inspire a commitment to people who are often left vulnerable by loss, poverty, displacement or difficult circumstances.

Our purpose is simple but profound: to restore hope, protect dignity and create practical pathways towards empowerment. As the Foundation enters a renewed chapter, our focus is on stronger governance, meaningful partnerships, community-based programmes and measurable impact.

"Restoring hope. Restoring dignity. Transforming lives."
What Drives Us

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Vision

To build communities where every widow, widower, orphan and vulnerable child lives with dignity, hope and equal opportunity.

Mission

To restore hope through practical support, empowerment, education, advocacy and sustainable community development.

Compassion Integrity Dignity Accountability Empowerment Service Excellence
Who We Serve

Standing with those who need it most

  • Widows and widowers facing economic or social vulnerability

  • Orphans and vulnerable children who need protection, care and opportunity

  • Young people who need mentorship, skills and pathways to development

  • Disadvantaged families affected by poverty, hardship or crisis

  • Communities requiring emergency or seasonal assistance
