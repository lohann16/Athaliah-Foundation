Our Foundation

Compassion that leads to lasting change

Athaliah Foundation was founded on a simple belief: grief and loss should never be faced alone, and no child should be left without a path forward. We work hand in hand with widows, widowers and orphaned children — providing care in the moment and the tools to build independent, dignified futures.

Compassion

Every family we meet is treated with warmth, patience and genuine care — never as a case number.

Dignity

We support in ways that respect people's independence and choices, not just their immediate needs.

Empowerment

From skills training to school fees, we invest in the tools that let families stand on their own.
